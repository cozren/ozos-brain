# Onboarding retro

**Date:** 2026-10-08

---

## What went well

- Clinics that imported their calendar on day one booked within 48 hours in every case we looked at.
- The new welcome call script cut back-and-forth email by half.

## What did not

- Calendar import still fails on two of the three common exports; clinics give up and wait for a call.
- Nobody owns the handover from sales to clinic success, so the first day is often lost.

## Actions

- Dana redesigns the import step with a visible fallback.
- Harper splits onboarding time by clinic size so we know where the four-day median comes from.
- Luca names an owner for the sales handover by next week.
